Our story

Built in Sivagangai, for Sivagangai

IRONHUB started as a place where people from town could train with real weights, honest coaching, and a community that shows up every day.

What we stand for

Strength is not a trend. It is showing up before work, loading the bar with respect, and leaving better than you walked in. We keep the music loud, the floor clean, and the coaching direct.

Whether you are preparing for police / army fitness, cricket off-season, or simply want to feel strong in daily life — this is your hub.
